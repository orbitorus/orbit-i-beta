Scoring Logic

How the Score is Calculated

The overall score is a weighted average of 6 category scores. Each category is built from individual rule checks. Full transparency — no black box.

Formula: Overall Score = Σ (Category Score × Weight) / Σ Weights
Each category score = average of its individual rule scores (0, 50, or 100 per rule).
PASS = 100 pts · PARTIAL = 50 pts · FAIL = 0 pts · N/A = excluded from average.

JAXA JMR-016 Collision Risk Levels
JMR-016 defines risk by combining Probability of Collision (Pc) and time remaining to TCA. Both parameters must be used — a high Pc far from TCA may not trigger action, while a moderate Pc close to TCA may. This is the operational standard for all JAXA-operated spacecraft and referenced by the Japan Cabinet Office licensing guidelines.

CA team reports to COLA team and CMO. All orgs monitor trend. No manoeuvre yet required.
LEVEL 2
URGENT
Pc ≥ 1×10⁻⁴ (N≤2 days) or Pc ≥ 1×10⁻⁵ (N>2 days) · Manoeuvrable spacecraft only
Primary decision meeting held. Manoeuvre plan prepared. Mandatory manoeuvre if Pc ≥ 1×10⁻³. Coordinate with secondary object operator if contactable.
LEVEL 3
CRITICAL
Pc ≥ 1×10⁻³ AND manoeuvre cannot be executed · Or: Pc ≥ 1×10⁻³ within 2 days TCA for non-manoeuvrable
Maximum possible action. Crisis Management Office fully activated. Public notification considered. Agency-wide response.
