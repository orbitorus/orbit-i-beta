## Strategic Scenarios

### Scenario A: Minimal Propulsion
- Saves $5M upfront
- Cannot handle traffic growth
- Total 10-year cost: ~$7M–$8M
- Catastrophic risk after Year 5

---

### Scenario B: High Propulsion
- $10M upfront investment
- Maintains maneuver capability
- Total 10-year cost: ~$13.9M
- Fully robust, path-independent

---

### Scenario C: Moderate Propulsion + ADR
- $5M propulsion + $5M ADR
- Balances cost and systemic risk reduction
- Total 10-year cost: ~$13.8M
- Optimal if ADR matures by ~2030

---

## Key Insights
1. Early propulsion investment prevents exponential risk growth
2. Design decisions are irreversible once launched
3. ADR provides system-wide benefits
4. Cheapest upfront option often leads to highest long-term losses
5. Insurance premiums escalate with traffic density

---

## Strategic Recommendation
- **Choose high propulsion** for dense shells and long missions
- **Choose moderate propulsion + ADR** if collective mitigation matures
- **Avoid minimal propulsion** unless mission ends before 2030

## Bottom Line
Tactical savings (delayed maneuvers) optimize costs per event.  
Strategic propulsion investment determines mission survival.
